Our Mission

CodeFUN was developed with a singular goal in mind: to make learning web design an easy and fun process for all ages. By "gamifying" lessons centering around the basics of HTML, learners of all ages will be able to develop their knowledge and skill while enjoying the process, and most importantly, having fun!
